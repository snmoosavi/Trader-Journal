# Trading Journal User Guide

Welcome to Trading Journal.

## Quick Start

1. Open the app.
2. Go to the Pre-Trade Gate tab and complete your checklist.
3. Use the Risk Calculator to calculate your position size.
4. Add your completed trades in the Journal tab.
5. Review your performance in the Reports tab.

## Micro and Mini Contracts

You can manually journal micro and mini futures trades by entering their symbols, such as MES, MNQ, ES, NQ, and similar contracts.

## CSV Import

CSV import/export can be used to move trade data in or out of the app. Future versions may improve support for broker/platform CSV formats.
